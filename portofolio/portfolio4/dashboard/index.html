ANALISIS DIAGNOSTIK AKAR MASALAH (ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS)
Metodologi: Descriptive & Diagnostic Cross-Tabulation
Sintesis Analisis Kualitas & Arahan Tindakan Korektif (CAPA)

Secara keseluruhan total reject rate sebesar 6,58%lebih dipengaruhi oleh karakteristik produk dan performa mesin dibandingkan faktor operator maupun shift. Pola time series menunjukkan data reject terus berfluktuasi secara berulang tanpa tren penurunan yang stabil. Selain itu penyebab reject di diagram Pareto cenderung merata tanpa ada yang dominan ekstrem. Maka tindakan perbaikan harus dilakukan pada banyak faktor sekaligus yaitu 7 parameter utama untuk mengejar target kumulatif 82%. Sedangkan investigasi teknisnya diprioritaskan pada kombinasi antara produk Yogurt dan Mesin M2 yang berkontribusi sebagai reject tertinggi sebesar 9,41%.

Executive Summary (English Translation)

Overall, the 6.58% reject rate indicates that quality variation is predominantly driven by product type, machine performance, and defect characteristics rather than operator or shift differences. Yogurt recorded the highest product reject rate (7.94%), while Machine M2 exhibited the highest equipment reject rate (7.13%), with the specific Yogurt–M2 interaction reaching 9.41%, warranting targeted engineering investigation.

Defect distributions vary significantly across product categories, with High Moisture, High pH, and Metal Detector Fail identified as the top three defect priorities, collectively accounting for 40% of total rejects. Trend analyses indicate recurring variation rather than a sustained quality trajectory. Consequently, corrective actions must prioritize process control in dosing and packaging operations, maintenance calibration for Machine M2, and systematic root-cause confirmation via the 5M1E framework.
